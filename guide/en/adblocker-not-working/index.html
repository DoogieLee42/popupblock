Ad Blocker Not Working on Safari iPhone? Here's Why

You installed an ad blocker, but Safari still opens shopping sites in new tabs, or sends you somewhere you never tapped. There are two very different reasons this happens, and the fix depends on which one it is.

Reason 1: the blocker is switched off somewhere

  1. Content blocker not enabled
    Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions. Content blockers have their own section; make sure yours is on.
  2. Turned off for this site
    In Safari, tap the page menu in the address bar › Website Settings. If "Use Content Blockers" is off, the blocker skips this site.
  3. Private Browsing
    Extensions are off in Private Browsing unless you allow it in the extension's settings.
  4. Outdated filter lists
    Open the blocker's app; most need to refresh their lists to catch new ads.
  5. Not in Safari
    Ads inside other apps, and pages opened in in-app browsers or Chrome, aren't covered by Safari extensions.

Reason 2: it's the wrong kind of blocker for this ad

Most "ad blockers" are content blockers. They keep a list of known ad addresses and stop those from loading. That works well for banners and trackers.

Pop-up and redirect ads work differently. They wait for your tap and open a new tab or move the page at that instant, often through the site's own address or a brand-new domain that isn't on any list yet. Because the tab opens right after a real tap, Safari's own Block Pop-ups setting lets it through too (the five tricks they use).

  • Banners, video ads, trackers → content blocker
  • New tabs on every tap, page swapped for an ad, sent to another site → pop-up and redirect blocking extension

They work in different ways, so you can run both side by side without conflicts.
