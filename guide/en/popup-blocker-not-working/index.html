Safari Pop-up Blocker Not Working on iPhone? Why Ads Still Get Through

You checked: Settings › Apps › Safari › Block Pop-ups is on. Yet some sites still open a new tab every time you tap. The blocker isn't broken; these ads are built to get around it.

What Block Pop-ups actually stops

Windows a page opens on its own: the moment it loads, after a timer, or while you're just scrolling. Those are reliably blocked.

What gets through, and why

Safari allows a window that opens right after you tap, because that's how real "open in new tab", sign-in and payment windows work. Aggressive ads hook into your tap:

  1. Invisible overlay: a transparent link covers the page, so any tap is a tap on the ad.
  2. Hijacked play button: the video's play button opens an ad tab first.
  3. Swapped link: a normal link changes its address at the moment you tap.
  4. Tab-under: your page opens in a new tab while the tab you were on is sent to the ad.
  5. Extra tab on a real link: you tap a genuine link and an ad tab opens alongside it.
  6. Fake close button: the X on an ad is itself a link to the ad (more).
  7. Redirect after loading: no tap at all; the page just navigates to an ad site a few seconds later. That's a redirect, not a pop-up, so Block Pop-ups doesn't cover it.

Checks worth doing first

  • Turn on Fraudulent Website Warning on the same Settings screen.
  • Look in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management for profiles you didn't install.
  • Review Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions for anything unfamiliar.
  • If it only happens on certain sites, it's those sites' ads, not your iPhone.

Stopping the ones that get through

These tricks can only be caught inside the page, at the moment of the tap: did you really tap a link, does the opening address match it, did your original tab move right after a new one opened? That's what a pop-up and redirect blocking Safari extension does, alongside Safari's own setting.
