Bank or Payment Window Won't Open in Safari? How to Fix It

You tap "Verify with your bank", "Pay now" or "Sign in with…", and nothing happens. Or a window flashes and disappears. Many banks and payment providers still open their verification step in a new window, and Safari can treat it as a pop-up.

Quick fixes, in order

  1. Tap the button once more. Safari lets through windows that open immediately after a tap. If the site opened it after a short delay, the first attempt was blocked; the second often works.
  2. On Mac, click the pop-up icon in the address bar to open the blocked window, or allow the site in Safari › Settings › Websites › Pop-up Windows (steps).
  3. On iPhone/iPad, switch Block Pop-ups off briefly (Settings › Apps › Safari), complete the payment, then switch it back on. There's no per-site option (why).
  4. Check your extensions. Ad and pop-up blockers can block the window too. Allow the site in the extension, or open it once from the extension's notice.
  5. Use the bank's app for the verification step if it offers one.

Still nothing?

  • Make sure the page is fully loaded before tapping; some payment buttons don't work until scripts finish loading.
  • Turn off "Hide IP Address" or a VPN briefly if the bank page itself won't load.
  • Clear that site's data: Settings › Apps › Safari › Advanced › Website Data, search for the site, and delete it. You'll be signed out of it.

Don't fall for fake "verification" windows

Real bank windows open when you tap a button on a site you trust. A window that appears by itself, says your account or iPhone has a problem, or asks you to call a number or install an app is almost certainly an ad or scam. Close it without tapping anything.
